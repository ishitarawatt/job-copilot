How it stays truthful: every tailored bullet must match one of your original bullets (80% word overlap or more) and may not add any number. Every claimed skill needs evidence in your resume. If the Critic rejects a draft, the Tailor gets one retry with the Critic's notes; if it still fails, the draft is withheld and sent for human review.
